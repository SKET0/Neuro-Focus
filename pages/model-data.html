Help train the Neuro-Focus attention model

This takes about 6 minutes. The screen will ask you to do a few short things: read some text, follow a dot, use your phone, look away and act tired. Each one lasts 30–40 seconds and each comes up twice.

No video is recorded. Your camera picture is analysed on this computer and thrown away straight away. Only numbers are kept, such as where your eyes point and the angle of your head, a few times a second.

The numbers are saved to a file on this computer when you finish. Nothing is uploaded. If you agree, send the file to the Neuro-Focus team. Don't type your name anywhere on this page: use the code you were given.

Before you start

  • Sit the way you normally would when studying, in your normal lighting.
  • Keep your phone nearby. You'll be asked to use it.
  • Make sure only your face is in view of the camera.
  • Turn your sound on. A beep tells you when to look back at the screen.

## Method

- Features per reading (8): gaze_x, gaze_y, eye_open, head_turn, face_size, head_yaw, head_pitch, head_roll
- Window: 16 readings (about 1.9 s), a new window every 4 readings; each task's first 1 s skipped; unusable readings are gaps, never inputs
- Model: LSTM (24 units) then one output (probability of focused); 40 epochs, Adam, learning rate 0.005, classes weighted equally; every training window also used left-right mirrored
- Screen edges: every focused training window also used turned up to 12° left or right and tilted up to 6° up or down (one random amount per copy), so looking at the edge of a large screen still counts as focused
- Calibration: face_size, eye_open, gaze_y, head_pitch measured against each person's median over their first 20 s looking at the screen
- Testing: Leave one person out (12 people)
- Rules: the app's current rule-based estimate (on-screen / looking-away) on the same windows

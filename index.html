01 Features

Everything a focused online classroom needs

Attention tracking, adaptive support and live classroom analytics, designed together for neurodivergent learners and the teachers who support them.

Real-time attention tracking

MediaPipe reads eye gaze and head pose in the browser and turns them into a live attention score every second.

Live classroom sessions

Teachers start a session in one click. Students get notified, join, and their focus streams to the teacher instantly.

Adaptive re-engagement

When focus slips, students get a gentle micro-task and a reminder of their next step, not a reprimand.

NeuroBuddy AI tutor

A patient study buddy that explains simply, summarises, quizzes and helps students get started when they’re stuck.

ADHD-friendly focus tools

Short focus blocks with breaks, micro-goals, a focus timeline and personal history that students own.

Teacher analytics

Attention curves, heatmaps, distraction alerts, saved session history and CSV reports for every class.

02 How it works

From a glance to an insight in under a second

All of the computer vision runs on the student’s own device. Only a handful of numbers ever travel to the teacher.

01

Camera stays local

The browser reads webcam frames on-device through the MediaStream API. Nothing is recorded.

02

Landmarks extracted

MediaPipe Face Mesh maps 478 points to measure gaze direction and head orientation.

03

Attention scored

A sliding window of behaviour is classified into Focused, Drifting or Distracted, with LSTM models in Phase 2.

04

Support in real time

Teachers see live scores and alerts; students get adaptive nudges and NeuroBuddy help.

04 Privacy by design

Attention insights without surveillance

Neuro-Focus was built so students can benefit from AI support without being recorded. The camera feed is processed on their device and immediately discarded; teachers only ever see numbers, and only during sessions students choose to join.

On-device processing

Face landmarks are computed in the browser. No video is uploaded or stored.

Student control

Sharing happens only in live sessions, and students can pause it anytime.

Private by default

Personal focus history is visible only to the student who owns it.

Secured access

Row-level security and private live channels keep class data inside the class.

05 Why it matters

Logins and clicks don’t measure attention

Students with ADHD struggle most with sustained focus in online classes, and in a crowded video call their distraction goes unnoticed until grades slip.

Typical e-learning platforms

  • Engagement judged by logins, clicks and minutes online
  • No live view of who is actually paying attention
  • One-size-fits-all design for neurotypical learners
  • Teachers find out only after assessments

With Neuro-Focus

  • Continuous, AI-based attention scoring from gaze and head pose
  • A live classroom dashboard with distraction alerts
  • Tools and nudges designed for neurodivergent learners
  • Early, supportive intervention while learning happens
06 FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Neuro-Focus record my webcam?

No. Video is analysed frame by frame inside your browser and never leaves your device. Only an attention score, a focus state and a session focus percentage are shared, and only during a live session you join.

Who can see my attention data?

During a live session, only the teacher of that class sees your live scores. Your personal history from tracking on your own is visible only to you. Classmates never see each other’s data.

What do I need to use it?

A laptop or desktop with a webcam and a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. There is nothing to install.

How do teachers and students connect?

Teachers create a class and share its join code. Students enter the code, and when the teacher starts a live session they get a notification to join.

Is this a medical or diagnostic tool?

No. Neuro-Focus supports attention during learning; it does not diagnose ADHD or any other condition.
